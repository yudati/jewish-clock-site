Privacy Policy

"Jewish Alarm Clock" (שעון יהודי) for Android (package: com.jewishalarmclock.app). Last updated: October 1, 2026. The Hebrew version of this policy is the primary one; this is a faithful translation.

In short: the app requires no sign-up, shows no ads and contains no tracking or analytics tools. Your settings, alarms and personal dates are stored on your device only. The only information that leaves the device is what's needed for a feature you asked for: your location when you look for a minyan, the text you typed when you search the books, and payment details when you choose to donate through Google Play.

1. Who we are

"Jewish Alarm Clock" is an independent Android app providing halachic times, a siddur, an alarm clock, a Hebrew calendar, a minyan finder and daily learning. It is operated by the app's developer ("we"). You can reach us at JewishAlarmClock@gmail.com.

2. What information is collected and why

We do not run servers of our own and receive no user information. The following is stored on your device only:

  • Settings and alarms — your chosen city, calculation method, siddur version, font, alarms, Shabbat mode and display preferences.
  • Personal dates — birthdays, anniversaries, yahrzeits and other dates you entered, for the calendar and reminders.
  • Your personal minyan list — synagogues and prayer times you added yourself.
  • Reading history and learning progress — "continue reading", Shnayim Mikra "done" marks, bookmarks.
  • Approximate location — if you allow location access, the app uses it to find the nearest locality for calculating times and to find a minyan nearby. Location is not stored as a history and is not sent to us.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, advertising IDs or usage data, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • Location — to find your locality for halachic times and nearby minyanim. You can decline and choose a city manually. Location is checked only while the app is open — never in the background.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, run at startup, full screen — so alarms and reminders fire on time, even when the device is locked or after a restart.
  • Ignore battery optimization — so the system doesn't delay alarms.
  • Calendar (read and write) — only if you turn on syncing holidays and personal dates to the device calendar. The app reads and writes only the events it created.
  • Internet — for the services listed in section 3.
  • Display over other apps — to show the ringing screen.
  • Vibrate, audio settings, wake lock — to play the alarm at the volume you set.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing) — for a voluntary one-time donation.

5. Storage, backup and deletion

  • All data is stored on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • If Android app backup is on, your settings may be backed up to your Google account by the operating system, encrypted, and restored on a new device. This backup is managed by Google under your device settings and is not accessible to us.
  • You can delete personal dates, personal minyanim and reading history inside the app, or clear all data via Settings › Apps › Jewish Alarm Clock › Storage.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since it collects no identifying details at all, we hold no data to delete; if you have any concern, please contact us.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, the rights to access, correct and delete are exercised directly on your device (see section 5). For any privacy question or request, we'll gladly help at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. A material change (for example, adding a service that receives information) will also be shown inside the app under "What's new".
